# ARIS-inspired strategy port
This is an independently written strategy, not a full ARIS run.
Map supplied literature into attempted mechanisms, unresolved assumptions, conflicting
observations and resource constraints. Generate a broad pool internally, then return
nonredundant candidates within quota. Consider cross-domain gaps, untested common
assumptions, contradictory findings and neglected operating regimes. At least one
candidate should have high informational upside even if its hypothesis fails.
Preserve named risks rather than watering down the mechanism. Provide the cheapest
experiment that discriminates the claim and precise prior-art search queries. Do not
claim a pilot, an independent reviewer, or a novelty search ran unless evidence says so.
