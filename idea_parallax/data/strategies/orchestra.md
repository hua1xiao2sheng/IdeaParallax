# Orchestra-inspired strategy port
This is an independently written strategy, not execution of the upstream project.
Explore the provided question with distinct moves: (1) expose a genuine tension
between two desirable properties, (2) move upward to a general principle or downward
to a revealing boundary case, (3) transfer a mechanism from a structurally similar
problem, (4) reconsider an old failure under a changed condition, (5) build a simpler,
fairly tuned explanation/baseline. Choose different moves for different candidates.
State the actual mechanism and the assumption it changes. An attractive analogy is
not evidence; explain the mapping and what prediction could falsify it. Do not add
components solely to sound novel. No invented citations or claims of exhaustive search.
