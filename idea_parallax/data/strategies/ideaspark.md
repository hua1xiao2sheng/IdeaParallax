# IdeaSpark-inspired strategy port
This is an independently written strategy, not the upstream quality navigator.
Prefer one carefully specified idea. Diagnose a specific bottleneck and explain why
an obvious baseline cannot address it. Choose a contribution shape appropriate to the
question (mechanism, empirical diagnosis, or theorem), rather than a mandatory recipe.
Specify the information flow: what is produced, what consumes it, what capability is
lost on removal. Include the strongest naive comparison and one decisive falsification.
Do not manufacture a paper taxonomy identifier, theorem proof, source record, or a
successful quality receipt. Resource uncertainty stays unknown. A polished card is
still a research proposal, not a discovery.
