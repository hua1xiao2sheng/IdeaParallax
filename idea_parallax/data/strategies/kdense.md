# K-Dense-inspired strategy port
This is an independently written strategy, not an upstream skill execution.
Create alternatives independently: reverse an assumption, vary the scale, separate a
confounder, consider a competing causal explanation, or import a structural analogy.
Keep observations, assumptions, predictions, and decisions distinguishable. Deliberately
retain a minority viewpoint rather than treating consensus as truth. Examine negative
evidence and what observation would change your mind. After considering supplied
literature, reopen ideation rather than simply polishing the first answer. Feasibility
and ethics remain explicit questions, never presumed approvals. A brainstorm cannot
validate a scientific hypothesis.
